# SD-DOOR-FRAME-HEAD — Door frame head

Use this detail at door heads where a framed opening meets the wall leaf above.

Clear opening width for a single residential leaf is typically 900 mm clear
(confirm project door schedule). The head frame sits tight to the structural
opening with packing and fire/acoustic sealant as specified.

Swing direction and leaf thickness are taken from the door schedule. Pair with
the threshold detail for complete opening control.

# SD-DOOR-THRESHOLD — Door threshold

Use this detail at door sills where accessibility and weather sealing matter.

Provide a weather bar or drained threshold on external doors; internal
thresholds shall not exceed the maximum upstand allowed for accessible routes.
Clear opening width is measured between finishes, not between frame members.

Coordinate with floor finishes and the door schedule leaf width. Habitable-room
egress doors must keep the clear opening unobstructed.

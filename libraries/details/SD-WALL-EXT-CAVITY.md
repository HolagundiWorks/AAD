# SD-WALL-EXT-CAVITY — External cavity wall

Use this detail for external brick cavity wall construction on plan and section.

Typical build-up: 230 mm outer brick leaf, 50 mm clear cavity with wall ties at
approved centres, insulated inner leaf, and a continuous damp-proof course at
floor level.

Cavity insulation shall be retained against the inner leaf; weep holes and
cavity trays are required above openings. Do not use this sheet for single-leaf
or drywall partitions.

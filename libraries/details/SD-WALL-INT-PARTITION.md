# SD-WALL-INT-PARTITION — Internal partition

Use this detail for non-loadbearing internal partitions between habitable rooms.

Typical options: 100 mm blockwork with plaster both sides, or metal stud with
drywall boards and mineral-wool sound insulation to the stated Rw rating.

Partition heads must allow for floor deflection. Wet areas require moisture-
resistant boards. Do not use this sheet for external walls or fire-rated shafts
without checking the fire schedule.
